Arctos Design System

This page documents the reusable, non-page-specific elements defined in styleguide.css (sourced from the Arctos site-wide stylesheet): color tokens, typography, notifications, links, buttons, status colors, form controls, tables, and small utilities. Page-specific layout rules (navbar, footer, modal overlays, the GUID page, SpecimenDetail, taxonomy search, the datatables search form) are intentionally out of scope — they depend on markup structures specific to those pages rather than being drop-in, reusable patterns.
